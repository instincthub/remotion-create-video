## LinkedIn Carousels

### Canvas

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Dimensions | 1080x1080px (square) or 1080x1350px (portrait) |
| Resolution | 72dpi minimum, 150dpi preferred |
| Export | PDF (carousel) or individual JPG/PNG per slide |
| Color mode | RGB |

### Slide Structure

**Cover (Slide 1):**
- Background: Brand Dark `#0F1A17` or Brand Teal `#1A7A6E`
- Headline: Nunito 700, white, 56-64px, max 8 words
- Subtext (optional): DM Sans 400, white 60% opacity, 20px
- Mark: white, bottom-right, 48px
- Max two text elements — must land in under 2 seconds

**Body (Slides 2-7):**
- Background: White or Paper
- Slide number: DM Mono 500, 11px, uppercase, teal, top-right
- Heading: Nunito 700, Charcoal, 28-32px
- Body: DM Sans 400, Charcoal, 16-18px, line-height 1.6
- One teal accent per slide (left border, underline, icon, or pill)
- Max 3-4 lines body text — split across slides if more needed

**Summary Slide:**
- Background: Brand Dark or white
- Key takeaway: Nunito 700, 28px
- CTA line: DM Sans 400, 16px
- Mark: always present

### Rules
- AI Playbook mark on every slide (bottom-right preferred)
- Consistent 48px margins on all sides
- Never change typeface or color within a carousel series

## Slide Decks (1920x1080, 16:9)

### Master Layouts

**Title Slide:**
- Background: Brand Dark
- Title: Nunito 700, 56px, white
- Subtitle: DM Sans 400, 24px, white 70% opacity
- Logo: horizontal lockup white/teal, bottom-left, 160px wide
- Session number: DM Mono 500, 13px, teal, top-right (e.g. SESSION 01)

**Content Slide:**
- Background: White
- Heading: Nunito 700, 36px, Charcoal
- Body: DM Sans 400, 20px, Charcoal, line-height 1.6
- Margins: 80px left, 60px top/bottom, 60px right
- Slide number: DM Mono 400, 12px, Neutral 400, bottom-right
- Logo mark: 32px, charcoal, bottom-right

**Section Divider:**
- Background: Teal `#1A7A6E`
- Title: Nunito 700, 64px, white
- Section number: DM Mono 500, 16px, white 60% opacity, above title

**Quote/Callout:**
- Background: Paper
- Quote: Nunito 600 italic, 36px, Charcoal
- Attribution: DM Sans 400, 18px, Teal
- Left border: 4px solid teal

### Deck Rules
- Max 5 words in slide title
- Max 4 bullets per slide, max 10 words per bullet
- One idea per slide
- Code examples: DM Mono in charcoal-background code block
- Diagrams: follow illustration style from imagery rules

## Brand Application Checklist

- [ ] Correct logo variant for the background
- [ ] Logo clearspace maintained
- [ ] All text uses Nunito, DM Sans, or DM Mono only
- [ ] No unapproved font weights
- [ ] All colors from approved palette
- [ ] Teal text on white/paper is 18px+ only
- [ ] Body text is DM Sans 400 or 500 (not Nunito)
- [ ] Photography shows diverse, real-world representation
- [ ] No generic AI stock imagery
- [ ] Screenshot annotations use brand style
- [ ] Margins meet minimum spec for format
- [ ] Certificates use correct tier spec
- [ ] Enterprise docs use approved cover template
