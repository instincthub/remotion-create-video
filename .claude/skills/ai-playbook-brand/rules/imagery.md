# AI Playbook — Imagery Standards

Source of truth: `docs/04 — Imagery`

---

## Photography

### Core Direction

Real, not staged. People actively working, building, thinking, and collaborating with technology.

### Use

- Professionals at work: laptops, screens, notebooks, whiteboards
- Groups collaborating in informal settings
- Close-ups of hands on keyboards, screens showing actual work
- African office environments: Lagos, Nairobi, Accra, Kigali, Cape Town, Abidjan
- Technology in context: AI tools on-screen, dashboards, actual UIs
- Real sessions and educators — not stock poses

### Avoid

- Generic stock: handshakes, glowing brains, robot hands, abstract AI art
- Exclusively non-African settings
- Overly polished lifestyle/aspirational advertising
- Text overlays conflicting with brand palette
- Blurred backgrounds with no context

### Color Treatment

- No heavy filters, saturation boosts, or vintage treatments
- Natural, true-to-life
- Subtle warm tone (+5 to +10) acceptable
- Full-bleed backgrounds behind text: 40-60% dark overlay

### Representation (Non-Negotiable)

- **Minimum 60%** African / Pan-African representation
- Geographic diversity across content series
- **Minimum 40%** women across image set
- Age range: 22-45 primary demographic

## Illustrations

### Diagram Illustrations (Educational)

- Flat, geometric, no drop shadows
- Palette: Brand Teal `#1A7A6E`, Charcoal `#2B3A35`, Neutral 200 `#E8E6DF`, White
- Stroke lines: 1.5-2px, charcoal or teal
- Rounded rectangle containers: 8px radius
- Arrows: open chevron head, 1.5px stroke
- All functional — no decorative illustrations

### Conceptual Illustrations (Social/Marketing)

- 2-3 colors maximum from brand palette
- Geometric shapes, not organic or hand-drawn
- No gradients, no 3D/isometric (unless concept requires it)
- Character illustrations: flat faces, no facial detail, diversity via neutral-scale skin tones

## Iconography

### Style

- Outline only (filled solids only under 16px)
- Stroke: 1.5px at 24px, 2px at 32px
- Corner radius: 2px (slightly rounded)
- Sizes: 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px — no arbitrary sizes
- Color: Charcoal on light, white on dark, Teal for active/highlighted states

### Libraries

- **Lucide Icons** — preferred (open source, outline, consistent stroke)
- **Heroicons** — acceptable alternative
- Never mix libraries in the same layout

### Custom Icons

- 24px base canvas
- 1.5px stroke, rounded caps and joins
- Export as SVG with no embedded fonts
- Color: `currentColor` for CSS recoloring

## Screenshot Standards

### Preparation

- Chrome or Firefox at 100% zoom, no extensions visible
- Window width: 1440px recommended
- Light mode system theme
- Remove personal data, bookmarks bar, irrelevant tabs

### Capture

- 2x resolution (Retina) preferred
- Minimum width: 1200px
- Save as PNG (never JPEG for text)

### Annotations

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Highlight box | 2px solid Teal `#1A7A6E`, no fill or 10% teal fill |
| Arrow | Teal `#1A7A6E`, 2px, open chevron head |
| Label text | DM Sans 500, 13px, charcoal on white pill background |
| Redaction bar | Charcoal `#2B3A35` filled rectangle (not black) |
| Number callout | 20px circle, teal fill, white Nunito 700 numeral |

### Framing

- 16px transparent margin when embedding
- LinkedIn carousels: annotate before placing in template
- Course modules: show full browser chrome for context

## Video Thumbnails

### Layout

- Background: Brand Dark `#0F1A17` or darkened photo with 50% dark overlay
- Text: Maximum 5 words, Nunito 700, white, 72-80px
- Accent: One teal element (underline, badge, or highlight block)
- Mark: AI Playbook mark-only, white, bottom-right, 48px

### Rules

- Never more than two lines of text
- Statements over questions (better performance)
- Sentence case only
- No red arrows, circles, or reaction faces
- Faces: confident, direct gaze — not pointing or reacting
- No face: meaningful screenshot or diagram from the lesson

---

## Never Use

- Generic AI stock (glowing brains, humanoid robots, circuit boards, holographic hands)
- Clip art or cartoon styles not matching brand illustration style
- Low-resolution images (under 72dpi screen, under 300dpi print)
- Watermarked stock images
- Images showing only one demographic exclusively
- Competitor platform screenshots as hero images without editorial justification
- Meme/viral formats in formal content (WhatsApp/Discord community only)
