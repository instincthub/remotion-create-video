Week of prompting with context

Quick AI tip to start the week👇

Most people prompt AI like they're texting a stranger.

"Write me a pitch."
"Summarise this."

The fix isn't a longer prompt. It's context.

Without context
"Write a LinkedIn post about AI."
With context
"Write a LinkedIn post for a fintech PM in Lagos. Audience: African banking execs. Tone: direct, no hype. Under 1000 characters."

Same task. Completely different output.
