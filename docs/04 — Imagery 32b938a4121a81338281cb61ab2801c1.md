# 04 — Imagery

> Imagery extends the brand's core character: practical, grounded, African-rooted, globally relevant. Every image used in AI Playbook content must be purposeful. Photography, illustration, iconography, and screenshots each have specific standards.
> 

---

## 4.1 Photography style

### Core direction

AI Playbook photography is real, not staged. It shows people actively working, building, thinking, and collaborating with technology. It does not show people posed in front of screens, holding tablets at impossible angles, or staring at cameras with perfect lighting and forced smiles.

### Subject matter

**Use:**

- Professionals at work on real tasks: laptops, screens, notebooks, whiteboards
- Groups collaborating in informal meeting settings
- Close-ups of hands on keyboards, screens showing actual work outputs
- African office and working environments: Lagos, Nairobi, Accra, Kigali
- Technology in context: AI tools on-screen, dashboards, actual application UIs
- Speakers and educators in front of audiences — real sessions, not stock poses

**Avoid:**

- Generic stock photos: handshakes, glowing blue brains, robot hands, abstract AI art
- Exclusively Western or non-African settings for a platform rooted in Africa
- Overly polished lifestyle imagery that reads as aspirational advertising
- Images with text overlay that conflicts with the brand colour palette
- Blurred backgrounds with no environmental context

### Colour treatment

- No heavy filters, saturation boosts, or vintage treatments
- Photography should feel natural and true-to-life
- A subtle warm tone (+5 to +10 warmth) is acceptable and consistent with the paper background palette
- Darken images used as full-bleed backgrounds behind text (overlay opacity 40–60% dark)

### Representation

Every image selection decision must actively include African professionals and settings. This is a non-negotiable differentiator. The AI Playbook is built in Africa. Its imagery must reflect that.

Target composition across a content piece or series:

- Minimum 60% of human subjects: African or Pan-African representation
- Geographic diversity: Lagos, Nairobi, Accra, Kigali, Cape Town, Abidjan
- Gender balance: minimum 40% women across the image set
- Age range: 22–45 as the primary target professional demographic

## 4.2 Illustration style

Illustrations are used for concept explanations, curriculum diagrams, social media carousels, and onboarding flows where photography cannot clearly convey the idea.

### Diagram illustrations (educational content)

- Flat, geometric, no drop shadows
- Colour palette: Brand Teal (#1A7A6E), Brand Charcoal (#2B3A35), Neutral 200 (#E8E6DF), White
- Thin stroke lines: 1.5–2px, charcoal or teal
- Rounded rectangle containers with 8px radius
- Arrow styles: open chevron head, 1.5px stroke
- No decorative illustrations as backgrounds — illustrations are functional, not decorative

### Conceptual illustrations (social / marketing)

- Minimal: 2–3 colours maximum from the brand palette
- Geometric shapes, not organic or hand-drawn
- No gradients
- No 3D rendering or isometric perspective unless a specific technical concept requires it
- Character illustrations (if used): flat faces, no facial detail, representing diversity in skin tone via a set of tonal variants from the neutral scale

## 4.3 Iconography

### Icon style

- Outline icons only — not filled solids unless used at very small sizes (under 16px)
- Stroke weight: 1.5px at 24px, 2px at 32px
- Corner radius: 2px on all right angles (slightly rounded, not sharp, not heavily rounded)
- Consistent sizing: 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px — never arbitrary sizes
- Colour: charcoal (#2B3A35) on light backgrounds, white on dark backgrounds, teal (#1A7A6E) for active or highlighted states

### Recommended icon library

- **Lucide Icons** (open source, outline style, consistent stroke) — preferred
- **Heroicons** (open source, outline and solid variants available)
- Do not mix icon libraries within the same layout

### Custom icons

When creating custom icons for the AI Playbook curriculum:

- Use a 24px base canvas
- 1.5px stroke, rounded caps and joins
- Export as SVG with no embedded fonts
- Colour should be currentColor (inherits from parent CSS) to allow recolouring

## 4.4 Screenshot standards

Screenshots are heavily used in AI Playbook content: tutorials, newsletter walk-throughs, LinkedIn carousels, and course modules. Poor screenshots are one of the most common quality failures in AI education content.

### Preparation before screenshotting

- Browser: Chrome or Firefox at 100% zoom, no extensions visible
- Browser window: maximised or at a consistent width (1440px recommended)
- System theme: light mode (consistent with brand palette)
- Remove personal data, bookmarks bar, and any irrelevant browser tabs
- Use a clean desktop background if the desktop is visible

### Capture settings

- Capture at 2× resolution (Retina) where possible — results in crisper text
- Minimum width: 1200px for full-page captures
- Save as PNG (never JPEG for screenshots — compression degrades text)

### Annotation style

When adding annotations to screenshots:

| Element | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Highlight box | 2px solid teal #1A7A6E, no fill or 10% teal fill |
| Arrow | Teal #1A7A6E, 2px, open chevron head |
| Label text | DM Sans 500, 13px, charcoal on white pill background |
| Redaction bar | Charcoal #2B3A35 filled rectangle — not black |
| Number callout | 20px circle, teal fill, white Nunito 700 numeral |

### Screenshot framing

- Add a 16px transparent margin around screenshots when embedding in documents
- For LinkedIn carousels: add the annotation before placing in the template — do not annotate after text overlay
- For course modules: always show the full browser chrome for orientation context

## 4.5 Video thumbnail style

YouTube thumbnails and video preview images follow a consistent template.

### Layout structure

- Background: Brand Dark (#0F1A17) or a darkened photograph with 50% dark overlay
- Text: Maximum 5 words, Nunito 700, white, 72–80px
- Accent: One teal element (underline, badge, or highlight block)
- Mark: AI Playbook mark-only in white, bottom-right corner, 48px

### Text rules

- Never more than two lines of text
- Avoid questions (statements perform better as YouTube thumbnails)
- Sentence case only
- Never use red arrows, circles, or reaction faces — they are off-brand

### Image rules

- If a face is shown: confident, direct gaze, not pointing or reacting
- If no face: a meaningful screenshot or diagram from the lesson
- Consistent colour temperature across the thumbnail series

---

## 4.6 What to never use

- Generic ‘AI’ stock imagery: glowing brains, humanoid robots, blue circuit boards, holographic hands
- Clip art or cartoon-style illustrations not matching the brand illustration style
- Low-resolution images (under 72dpi for screen, under 300dpi for print)
- Images with embedded watermarks from stock sites
- Images that show only one demographic or gender exclusively
- Brand-name competitor platform screenshots as hero images (Coursera, Udemy, etc.) without explicit editorial justification
- Meme formats or viral internet images in formal content (acceptable only in informal WhatsApp/Discord community contexts)
