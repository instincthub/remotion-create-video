## 5.1 LinkedIn carousels

LinkedIn carousels are the highest-reach format in the AI Playbook content mix. They must be visually consistent, immediately scannable, and on-brand across all 8–10 slides.

### Canvas specification

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Dimensions | 1080 × 1080px (square) or 1080 × 1350px (portrait) |
| Resolution | 72dpi minimum, 150dpi preferred |
| Format | Export as PDF (carousel) or individual JPG/PNG per slide |
| Colour mode | RGB |

### Slide structure

**Cover slide (Slide 1):**

- Background: Brand Dark (#0F1A17) or strong teal (#1A7A6E)
- Headline: Nunito 700, white, 56–64px, maximum 8 words
- Subtext (optional): DM Sans 400, white 60% opacity, 20px
- AI Playbook mark: white, bottom-right, 48px
- No more than two text elements — the cover must land in under 2 seconds

**Body slides (Slides 2–7):**

- Background: White (#FFFFFF) or Paper (#F4F2ED)
- Slide number: DM Mono 500, 11px, uppercase, teal, top-right
- Heading: Nunito 700, Charcoal, 28–32px
- Body: DM Sans 400, Charcoal, 16–18px, line-height 1.6
- Accent: one teal element per slide (left border, underline, icon, or pill)
- Max body text: 3–4 lines. If more is needed, split across two slides.

**Summary slide:**

- Background: Brand Dark or white
- Key takeaway: Nunito 700, 28px
- CTA line: DM Sans 400, 16px (e.g. “Follow for weekly AI Playbook sessions”)
- AI Playbook mark: always present

### Brand elements on every slide

- AI Playbook mark in the same corner on every slide (bottom-right preferred)
- Consistent margin: 48px on all sides minimum
- Never change the typeface or colour within a carousel series

## 5.4 Slide decks (course sessions and enterprise presentations)

### Canvas

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Dimensions | 1920 × 1080px (16:9) |
| Software | Google Slides, PowerPoint, or Keynote |
| Resolution | 150dpi for PDF export |

### Master layouts

**Title slide:**

- Background: Brand Dark (#0F1A17)
- Title: Nunito 700, 56px, white
- Subtitle: DM Sans 400, 24px, white 70% opacity
- Logo: horizontal lockup white/teal, bottom-left, 160px wide
- Session number: DM Mono 500, 13px, teal, top-right (e.g. SESSION 01)

**Content slide:**

- Background: White
- Heading: Nunito 700, 36px, Charcoal
- Body: DM Sans 400, 20px, Charcoal, line-height 1.6
- Margin: 80px left, 60px top/bottom, 60px right
- Slide number: DM Mono 400, 12px, Neutral 400, bottom-right
- Logo mark: 32px, charcoal, bottom-right (5px above slide number)

**Section divider slide:**

- Background: Teal (#1A7A6E)
- Section title: Nunito 700, 64px, white
- Section number: DM Mono 500, 16px, white 60% opacity, above the title

**Quote / callout slide:**

- Background: Paper (#F4F2ED)
- Quote: Nunito 600 italic, 36px, Charcoal
- Attribution: DM Sans 400, 18px, Teal
- Left border: 4px solid teal

### Deck rules

- Maximum 5 words in a slide title
- Maximum 4 bullet points per slide; maximum 10 words per bullet
- One idea per slide — split if two ideas are competing
- Every code example uses DM Mono in a charcoal-background code block
- Diagrams follow the illustration style from Section 4.2

## 5.7 WhatsApp and Discord (community channels)

These channels are informal but should still reflect the AI Playbook identity.

### Community profile images

- WhatsApp: mark-teal.png (500 × 500px)
- Discord: mark-dark.png (512 × 512px)

### Announcement graphics

When sharing image-based announcements in community channels:

- Use a simplified version of the LinkedIn carousel cover template
- 1080 × 1080px, Brand Dark background, Nunito headline, teal accent
- Always include the AI Playbook mark
- Keep text to a maximum of 12 words

### Text formatting in community posts

- Use *bold* sparingly for key information
- Use ALL CAPS only for single words to draw attention (not full sentences)
- Never post walls of text — use short paragraphs with line breaks
- Week numbers and dates in DM Mono style: WK 07, MONTH 2 (write as plain text in chat)

## 5.8 Brand application checklist

Before publishing or distributing any AI Playbook branded asset, confirm:

- [ ]  Logo is the correct approved variant for the background colour
- [ ]  Logo clearspace is maintained on all sides
- [ ]  All text uses Nunito, DM Sans, or DM Mono only
- [ ]  No font weight outside the approved set is used
- [ ]  All colours are from the approved brand palette
- [ ]  Teal text on white/paper is 18px or larger only
- [ ]  Body text is DM Sans 400 or 500 only (not Nunito)
- [ ]  All photography shows diverse, real-world representation
- [ ]  No generic AI stock imagery is used
- [ ]  Annotations on screenshots use the correct brand annotation style
- [ ]  Document or slide margins meet the minimum spec for the format
- [ ]  Any certificate uses the correct tier specification
- [ ]  Enterprise documents use the approved cover template
