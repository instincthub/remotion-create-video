## VIDEO SCRIPT

What if I told you Artificial Intelligence is not just about chatbots and image generators?

Some companies are saving millions.
Cutting thousands of manual hours.
And reducing errors at a massive scale.

Let us look at what real enterprise Artificial Intelligence actually does.

First, financial accounting.

Every business transaction must be categorised.
Into the right account.
Using something called a Chart of Accounts.

Now imagine millions of small businesses.
Billions of transactions.
Even if categorising one transaction takes three seconds,
that becomes over a thousand person-years of work every single year.

So what did they build?

A recommender system.

Not a generic one.
But one that understands each user’s custom categories.

It looks at how similar users categorised similar transactions.
Then predicts the most likely account for a new transaction.

That is supervised learning.
Working quietly in the background.

The result?

Fewer uncategorized transactions.
Fewer errors.
Less manual cleanup.
And billions of transactions are processed with higher accuracy.

Now, let us move to manufacturing.

A factory producing weld nuts.
Small components.
But critical.

Inspection was manual.
Dependent on human focus.
And humans get tired.

The requirement?
Classify each item in less than 0.2 seconds.
With at least 95 percent accuracy.

So they used a Convolutional Neural Network.

Image goes in.
Preprocessing happens.
The model classifies the weld nut.
Non-defective.
Or one of seven defect categories.

All in about 0.14 seconds.
With over 99 percent accuracy.

That is computer vision in production.
Not in a lab.
Not in a demo.
On a factory floor.

And the business result?
Lower labour cost.
Lower failure cost.
Real monthly financial benefit.

Now, let us shift to government procurement.

A large research organisation wanted to reduce spending.
Bulk buying.
Better vendor negotiations.
Lower shipping costs.

The data was messy.
Many items.
Many vendors.
Hidden relationships.

So they modelled procurement data as a graph.

Items connected to vendors.
Vendors connected to contracts.

Then they applied clustering.
An unsupervised learning technique.

The system identified demand aggregation patterns.
Opportunities to combine purchases.
Negotiate better bulk contracts.

The outcome?
Millions in annual savings.

Now here is the deeper pattern.

Enterprise Artificial Intelligence is not about flashy demos.

It is about systems.

Data.
Models.
Integration.
Workflow redesign.

And often, hybrid systems.

Take enterprise risk management.

Traditionally, scenario planning required thousands of human hours.
Experts reading reports.
Imagining risks.
Manually mapping cause and effect.

The Artificial Intelligence solution used two components.

First, Natural Language Processing.
To extract causal relationships from authoritative documents.

Second, an Artificial Intelligence planning engine.
To simulate hundreds or thousands of future scenarios.

The result?

What used to take thousands of person-hours
can now be generated in hours.

Not replacing experts.
But augmenting them.

Here is the key insight.

Enterprise Artificial Intelligence works when:

It solves a clear business bottleneck.
It integrates into existing systems.
It delivers measurable outcomes.

Fewer errors.
Faster processing.
Lower cost.
Better foresight.

So next time you hear the word Artificial Intelligence,
do not think hype.

Think systems.
Think workflow.
Think measurable impact.

Because in the enterprise world,
Artificial Intelligence is not magic.

It is disciplined engineering applied to business reality.
