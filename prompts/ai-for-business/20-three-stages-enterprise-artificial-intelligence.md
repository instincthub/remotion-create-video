Video Title: The 3 Stages of Enterprise Artificial Intelligence
Slug: three-stages-enterprise-artificial-intelligence
Excerpt: Building an Artificial Intelligence model is only the beginning. To create real business value, enterprises must develop, deploy, and sustain their systems with rigorous data processes and long term monitoring.

## VIDEO SCRIPT

Most teams think that building the Artificial Intelligence application is the hard part.
It is not!

The real challenge begins after development.
Because enterprise Artificial Intelligence is not just code.
It is data.
And data changes.

Let us break this down into three stages.

Stage one. Develop.

This is where you collect real-world data.
Not perfect data.
Messy data.

You filter it.
Clean it.
Remove duplicates.
Combine it with other sources.

Data scientists do a lot of this manually.
But here is the problem.

Whatever you clean in development must also be cleaned in production.

If you manually fix the data during training but do not automate that same process when the system goes live, your model will fail.

Development also requires proper testing.
You cannot train and test on the same data.
You must hold out a subset for validation.
Otherwise, you are fooling yourself.

Stage two. Deploy.

Now your model leaves the lab.

It connects to real systems.
Databases.
APIs.
Enterprise software.

This stage requires different skills.
Security reviews.
Ethics checks.
Archival standards.
Integration testing.

Your data scientists alone cannot carry this.

Deployment is where many projects slow down.
Because production environments are stricter than experiments.

Stage three. Sustain.

And this is the part most teams underestimate.

Traditional software waits for errors.
Artificial Intelligence must be monitored for drift.

Imagine your system was trained on English text.
Then suddenly it starts receiving French or mixed language input.

The model may not crash.
But performance drops.

Entity counts change.
Confidence scores shift.
Patterns look different.

That is data drift.

Sustaining means continuously monitoring data statistics.
Tracking performance.
Retraining when needed.

And here is the hard truth.

This may happen years later.
Your original developers may be gone.

If you documented everything.
If you built automated pipelines.
If you created a dedicated monitoring team.

Recovery is simple.

If you hacked it together just to ship fast.

It becomes expensive.

Enterprise Artificial Intelligence is not built and forget.
It is built.
Deploy.
Sustain.

Tools are temporary.
Data moves.
Systems evolve.

Developers build models.
Enterprises build processes.

If you want business value from Artificial Intelligence.
Do not just ship a model.

Design for the full lifecycle.
