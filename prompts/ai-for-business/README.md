# Course Summaries

We've put together two courses designed to help your team work smarter with AI.

## AI for Business (21 lessons)

Learn why 80% of enterprise AI projects fail and how to beat those odds. This course covers AI's evolution, the real differences between consumer and enterprise AI, and what it takes to build systems that actually work in business. Through real-world case studies in content moderation, financial categorization, manufacturing, and government procurement, you'll gain a practical framework for introducing, deploying, and sustaining AI across your organization.

### Module 1: AI Foundations & Evolution (Lessons 1–9)

Covers the introduction to AI in business, AI winters, why AI projects fail, why AI applications are different, board games that shaped AI, NLP evolution, expert systems, and robot autonomy.

Sets context — 80% of enterprise AI projects fail, and this module explains why by tracing AI's history, breakthroughs, and recurring pitfalls.

### Module 2: The Enterprise AI Challenge (Lessons 10–16)

Covers AI vs smart automation, why web giants dominate AI, enterprise complexity, the expectations gap, real AI applications, real-world systems, and multi-component systems.

Consumer AI recommends movies. Enterprise AI approves loans and diagnoses patients. This module covers why enterprise AI is fundamentally harder — and how real organizations are making it work.

### Module 3: Building & Scaling Enterprise AI (Lessons 17–21)

Covers the A-to-I framework, complexity at scale, DevOps and architecture challenges, the three stages of enterprise AI, and readiness assessment.

Knowing AI is hard isn't enough — you need a system. This module walks through the full enterprise AI lifecycle: where to introduce AI, how to architect it, and how to sustain it at scale.

## Introduction to Prompt Engineering

Learn how to get better results from AI tools your team is already using. This course covers how AI models interpret prompts, the structure of effective prompts (instructive, descriptive, role-based, and contextual), best practices for clarity and iteration, common mistakes to avoid, and key considerations around compliance, data security, and ethical use.

### Module 4: What Is Prompt Engineering (Lessons 1–3)

Covers what prompt engineering is, real-world applications in chatbots, risk analysis, and report automation, plus an overview of AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

AI tools are only as good as the prompts you give them. This module introduces prompt engineering and shows how it already powers chatbots, risk analysis, and automated reporting across industries.

### Module 5: How AI Reads Your Prompts (Lessons 4–6)

Covers how AI models interpret prompts, basic prompt structure (clear objective, context, specific instructions), and types of prompts — instructive, descriptive, and conversational.

A great prompt isn't just a question — it's a structure. This module breaks down how AI models interpret input and introduces the building blocks of effective prompts: objectives, context, and specific instructions.

### Module 6: Advanced Prompt Techniques (Lessons 7–9)

Covers role-based prompts, contextual prompts, and best practices including simplicity, clarity, and iterative testing.

Generic prompts get generic answers. This module teaches you to use role-based and contextual prompting to get sharper, more targeted AI responses — and how to refine them through iteration.

### Module 7: Responsible AI & Prompt Pitfalls (Lessons 10–12)

Covers common mistakes like over-engineering and lack of clarity, compliance and data security when using AI, and ethical considerations including bias in AI decision-making.

Powerful prompts come with responsibility. This module covers the most common prompt engineering mistakes, how to handle sensitive data securely, and how to address bias in AI outputs.
