Video Title: What Should a Vibe Coder Actually Learn?
Slug: what-should-a-vibe-coder-learn
Excerpt: If you can build apps with prompts but do not fully understand how things work under the hood, this video breaks down exactly what a vibe coder must learn to move from experimentation to real engineering confidence.

## VIDEO SCRIPT

You can build an app in thirty minutes with prompts.

But here is the real question.

If it breaks… can you fix it?

Right now, thousands of vibe coders are shipping projects fast. Landing clients. Launching MVPs. Building startups.

And that is amazing.

But speed without understanding is fragile.

Because when the API fails…
When authentication breaks…
When your state becomes chaos…
You cannot prompt your way out of everything.

So what should a vibe coder actually learn?

Let us break it into three parts.

First, what you need to know.
Second, what you need to see.
And third, what you need to do.

What You Need to Know

You need to understand layers.

Every modern app has structure.

Frontend.
Backend.
Database.
APIs.
Hosting.

When you use Artificial Intelligence to generate code, it is assembling these layers for you. But if you do not understand what each one does, you are just stacking invisible blocks.

Next, you must understand data flow.

A user clicks a button.
The frontend sends a request.
The backend processes it.
The database stores or retrieves data.
A response comes back.

If you cannot trace that journey clearly in your head, debugging becomes guesswork.

You also need to understand that prompts are instructions, not intelligence.

Artificial Intelligence predicts patterns.
It does not understand your long term architecture.
It does not think strategically about scalability.

It reflects the clarity of your thinking.

And finally, you must learn fundamentals.

What is a REST API?
What is state management?
What is authentication?
What is latency?
What is version control?

You do not need to master everything immediately.

But you must understand the concepts.

Because fundamentals create control.

What You Need to See

I want you to see the invisible system behind your code.

Picture this.

A simple button on your screen.

When it is clicked, a signal travels to a server.
The server talks to a database.
The database responds.
And data flows back to the user.

Now imagine one connection failing.

That is where your bug lives.

I want you to see that Artificial Intelligence is not replacing engineering.

It is accelerating it.

It is like a power tool.

A power tool makes you faster.

But you still need to know where to drill.

And I want you to see yourself differently.

Not as someone who copies and pastes.

But as a systems thinker.

Someone who designs flows.
Designs tradeoffs.
Designs decisions.

That mindset shift changes everything.

What You Need to Do

Here is your action plan.

First, rebuild something simple without Artificial Intelligence.

A to do app.
A small API.
A basic login system.

Feel the friction.

That friction teaches architecture.

Second, when you use Artificial Intelligence, interrogate it.

Ask it to explain every major block of code.

Why is this here?
What happens if I remove this?
Is there a cleaner way?

Do not copy. Understand.

Third, practice debugging.

Break things intentionally.

Change values.
Trigger errors.
Read stack traces.

Confidence comes from knowing you can fix what breaks.

Fourth, deploy something small.

Push an app live.

Learn environment variables.
Learn build processes.
Understand what happens between your laptop and the internet.

Because that journey is real engineering.

Here is the truth.

A vibe coder experiments.

A real builder understands.

Artificial Intelligence is your amplifier.

But fundamentals are your foundation.

If you learn how it works, you do not just build faster.

You build with control.

And control is what turns a trend into a career.
