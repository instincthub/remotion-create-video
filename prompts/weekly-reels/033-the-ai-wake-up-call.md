## VIDEO SCRIPT

**Episode 1: "The Artificial Intelligence Wake-Up Call"**

---

Your child is sitting in a classroom right now.

And somewhere in the world, a company just replaced an entire team of entry-level workers with Artificial Intelligence.

These two facts are connected. And most parents have no idea.

Here is something that should stop you in your tracks. The World Economic Forum says Artificial Intelligence will reshape 22% of all jobs by 2030. That is not a distant future. That is before most children in school today will even start their careers.

And yet, nearly half of all parents have never had a single conversation with their child about it.

Not one.

Let that sink in.

We are living through the fastest technological shift in human history. Artificial Intelligence coding benchmarks improved sixteenfold in just twelve months, from 2023 to 2024. What took a decade in previous tech revolutions is now happening in a single year.

But here is what makes this moment truly urgent.

A Samsung and Morning Consult survey found that 88% of parents believe Artificial Intelligence knowledge will be critical for their child's future. 88%. That is nearly every parent in the room.

But 81% of those same parents do not believe Artificial Intelligence is even being taught in their child's school.

That gap, between knowing something matters and actually doing something about it, that is the gap that will define your child's future.

Parents are worried. And they should be. But not for the reasons they think.

65% of parents fear Artificial Intelligence will destroy their child's ability to think critically. More than half fear it will make it harder for their kids to find jobs. 44% of parents across 19 countries say they simply do not have the knowledge to guide their children's Artificial Intelligence use at all.

These are real fears. But fear without direction is just paralysis.

And here is the uncomfortable truth about schools.

Only 19% of schools have a formal Artificial Intelligence policy. 68% of teachers received zero Artificial Intelligence training this past school year. Zero. And yet 85% of those same teachers were already using Artificial Intelligence tools in their classrooms, most of them without any guidance at all.

The institutions we trust to prepare our children are themselves unprepared.

So who is filling that gap?

Right now, nobody. And that is exactly why you are watching this.

The World Economic Forum projects that Artificial Intelligence will create 78 million net new jobs this decade. 78 million. But only for people who are equipped with the right skills. The opportunity is enormous. But it will not wait.

Countries like Finland, Estonia, China, and the United Arab Emirates are already teaching Artificial Intelligence literacy from kindergarten. The UAE has made it a mandatory subject from age 4 through Grade 12. China saw 80% of its students report excitement about Artificial Intelligence. The United States? 35%.

This is not just an education problem. It is a preparation problem. And it starts at home.

Over the next seven episodes, we are going to give you everything you need. Which jobs are disappearing, which ones are being created, what skills your child actually needs, what you can do tonight, what schools must change, age-by-age tools and activities, and how to have this conversation with your child without creating fear.

Because the families who understand what is coming, those are the families who will turn this disruption into the greatest opportunity of their children's lives.

The Artificial Intelligence wake-up call is ringing.

This series is your answer.
