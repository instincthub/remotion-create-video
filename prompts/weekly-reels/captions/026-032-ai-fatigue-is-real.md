AI fatigue is real — and nobody's talking about it.

We upgraded our plans. We spun up 7-8 projects at once. We burned through tokens like they were unlimited.

And yet... we were going in circles.

Here's what I learned the hard way:

1. Timebox your AI sessions — 25 min coding, 10 min thinking, 5 min break. Repeat.

2. Right task, right tool — Let AI handle boilerplate, docs, and tests. YOU own the architecture, design systems, and domain logic.

3. Watch your cognitive load — If you're prompting in circles without progress, stop. Take a break. Try a different approach.

4. Go deep — 40% of AI-generated code has vulnerabilities. Don't just vibe code — understand what's been built. Own the logic.

5. Stop switching — Pick one AI per purpose and stick with it. Claude for coding. ChatGPT for writing. Gemini for research. Categorize, don't scatter.

The goal isn't to write more code.
The goal is to build better systems — without burning out.

AI should assist your thinking, not replace it.
If you're more productive but less fulfilled, the flow is wrong.
