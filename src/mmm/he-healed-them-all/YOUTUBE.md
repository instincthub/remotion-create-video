## Description

Beloved, sometimes in life we need something — Someone — greater than all.
In this Monday Morning Missive, Hon. Justice Oluyinka Gbaja-Biamila opens the
Scriptures on the healing power of Jesus Christ, the Balm in Gilead, "who went
about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil."

Whatever your affliction — emotional, physical, or spiritual — the peerless
name of Jesus is available to you today. Walk through the Gospels and the Acts
of the Apostles and see, again and again, that He healed them all.

📖 Scriptures referenced in this message:
• Matthew 12:15 — "And he healed them all."
• Acts 10:38 — Anointed with the Holy Ghost and with power
• Malachi 4:2 — The Sun of righteousness with healing in His wings
• John 14:13 — "Whatsoever ye shall ask in my name, that will I do."
• Acts 3:6 — "In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk."
• Luke 4:38–39 — Peter's mother-in-law healed
• Luke 8:43–48 — The woman with the issue of blood
• John 11:43–44 — "Lazarus, come forth."

Call on Him today. Ask Jesus to come into your life, to heal that disease, that
sickness, that emotional turmoil — and believe: you have a story to tell, and a
testimony to give. God bless you all.

🔔 New Monday Morning Missive every Monday morning. Subscribe and turn on
notifications so you never miss a word.

🙏 If this message blessed you, share it with someone who needs healing today,
and leave a comment with your prayer request or testimony.

composited frame-by-frame, so this takes a while but is straightforward at
1080p):

```

```

**Thumbnail** (1920×1080 PNG):

```
npx remotion still mmm-hhta-thumbnail out/mmm-hhta-thumbnail.png

```
