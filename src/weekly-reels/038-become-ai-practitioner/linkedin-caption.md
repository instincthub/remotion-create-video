# LinkedIn Caption — 038 Become an AI Practitioner

---

Most of us already use AI. Very few have a path with it.

There are 5 career paths into practical AI. Here are the first two.

**Path 01 — Consumer**
You're already on this one. Gmail filters your inbox. Netflix recommends what to watch. ChatGPT drafts your emails and marketing plans. Using the product is a real path. It just ends there.

**Path 02 — Explorer**
You go past the surface. You study your business domain. You find the manual processes that take weeks and rebuild them to run in minutes. You become the person your team asks before the next role is even scoped.

The difference between the two isn't talent. It's clarity and strategy.

Paths 03, 04, and 05 are coming in the next reel.

Which one describes you right now — Consumer or Explorer?
